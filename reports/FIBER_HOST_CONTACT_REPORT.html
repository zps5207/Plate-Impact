Discovery of the Own-Ply VUINTERACTION Explosion:
The implementation of fiber-host contact in nv3_fh triggered a catastrophic kinetic energy explosion starting at $t pprox 1.48$ μs, with total energy surging from 469.2 J to 25,710 J at 1.90 μs. Diagnostic bisection proved conclusively that the 380 native other-ply contact pairs (Run A) are 100% stable, whereas the 20 own-ply user interaction pairs (Run B) caused the runaway. 1. Motivation & Background (T-056 Design)

In the NV3 plate, fibers embedded at the mid-plane of each ply have an equivalent truss diameter of 0.632 mm. Mid-ply host hex faces sit 0.212 mm from the fiber nodes, creating an initial geometric overlap of 0.104 mm. Standard penalty contact cannot be used on own-ply hosts because this initial overlap would release massive artificial strain energy at $t=0$.

Task T-056 proposed a custom user subroutine VUINTERACTION implementing a baseline ratchet law: store the initial penetration d_0 = min(d_0, max(d, 0)) and apply compressive force only when dynamic penetration exceeds d_0. Fibers in other plies contacted hosts via native penalty contact.

3. Root Cause: Diagnostic Analysis & Coupon Verification

ODB Diagnostic Findings (diag_nv3_fh.txt)

  • The explosive kinetic energy at $t=1.48$ μs was localized in single orphan host nodes of PLY-20 on the symmetry plane ($y=0$).
  • Node 720 accelerated to 228,800 m/s, carrying 393 J of kinetic energy in a single nodal mass ($1.5 imes 10^-8$ kg).
  • Subsequently, node 10859 accelerated to 1,460,000 m/s.

Physical Coupon Verification (Hub cx057 & cx057b Runs)

Investigation T-057 proposed several initial hypotheses (H1–H7). The Hub physically verified and refined these mechanisms on ROAR (verification/roar/fiberhost_t057/, jobs 57328611 and 57336115; source: project notes):

  • Agy Claim Correction (Force Math): Agy's T-057 report suggested a 12.5 N contact force caused node 720's runaway. Hub review noted this was off by 1000×: accelerating 1.5 × 10-8 kg to 228 km/s in 38 ns requires approximately 4 × 104 N, not 12.5 N.
  • Agy Claim Correction (AREAPROX): In Abaqus 2024, AREAPROX is dimensionless (values between 0.12 and 0.64), serving as a weight factor, NOT contact area in m². Multiplying contact pressure by AREAPROX as area produced unphysical force estimates.
  • Coupon cx057 (Embedded Fibers): When fibers were embedded in host elements, VUINTERACTION had zero calls! Abaqus general contact automatically skips facets of the element an embedded node sits in.
  • Coupon cx057b (Non-Embedded Fibers): Yielded 12,430 log lines upon element deletion. Key proven findings:
    1. Exactly 4 calls per increment (273 of 273 increments); the underlying reason for 4 calls per increment is not established.
    2. State is NOT fresh per new node-facet pair: Master facets paired later arrived with st2in=1 and d0=0, proving that slave node state is corrupted across multiple facets.
    3. PENETRATION values did not correspond to geometric overlaps.

4. Production Decision & nv3_fhA100 Candidate

Because own-ply contact is physically redundant with *EMBEDDED ELEMENT while elements are intact, and geometrically ill-posed once host elements erode, the decision was made to drop own-ply fiber-host contact entirely.

Production candidate nv3_fhA100 (incorporating the proven Run A native other-ply contact for all $j e k$) was submitted to ROAR:
