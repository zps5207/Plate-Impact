Vals plywise rerun with element deletion on for all elements

Same 20-ply Vals deck as the first pair, ball at -1000 m/s, step 7e-5 s, 200 exported frames. Change: *Damage Evolution added to the fiber and steel ball materials and *Section Controls element deletion=YES on every section (build_vals_plywise.py --element-deletion). ROAR jobs 56580053 (VUEL) and 56580055 (native), directory ctv/vp4. Both datachecks and both solves completed with exit code 0.

Summary

  • Both solves completed the full 70 us (datacheck accepted *Damage Evolution on the T3D2 fibers).
  • The fiber blow-up is largely gone. Peak ALLKE is about 5.8 J (VUEL) and 6.1 J (native), against 366 J and 56 J in the first pair. Maximum host node speed fell to about 35 km/s (VUEL, was 77) and 23 km/s (native, was 42).
  • The energy balance still does not close. ALLKE ends 63% (VUEL) and 73% (native) above the 3.5 J the ball starts with, so energy is still being created somewhere.
  • The ball is barely slowed: about 963 m/s (VUEL) and 968 m/s (native) at the end, with under 1% of host elements deleted. This is the opposite of the first pair, where the ball kept 742 and 669 m/s. I have not established why. Ball elements can now erode, so the ball centre is no longer a clean measure; treat the velocity as unconfirmed.
  • VUEL and native now agree closely on velocity, deletion and energy, much closer than before.

Caveats

  • Ball nose z is the minimum node z, so it jumps when ball elements erode and their nodes are ejected (nose ends at -85 mm VUEL, -243 mm native while the centre is near -57 mm). Use the centre only.
  • Fiber and ball deletion state is not exported, only positions, so how many fibers actually failed is not measured here.
  • Energy not conserved: the likely sources (mass of deleted nodes, excessive distortion control, contact) were not investigated in this run.
