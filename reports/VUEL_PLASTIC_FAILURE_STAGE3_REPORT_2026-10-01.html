Stage 3 report: Jacobian (collapse) failure trigger (2026-10-01)

Branch: vuel-plastic-failure. Adds props(11)=JAC_FAIL and the min(det(F))-over-8-Gauss-points collapse trigger to uel/VUEL_failure.for, alongside (not replacing) the existing engineering-strain trigger. Rationale (already flagged in docs/VUEL_PLASTIC_FAILURE_STAGE0_PLAN.md): 1-D engineering strain is bounded at -1 under compression, so the goal's suggested EPS_FAIL~1.0 is structurally a tension-side-only trigger; compression/shear collapse needs a geometric (volume-ratio) measure instead.

Implementation

  • DETF (= det(F), the current/reference volume ratio at a Gauss point) was already computed every call by COMPUTE_DEFGRAD; DETJ0 (the reference-configuration Jacobian from SHAPE8H_TL) is a different, constant quantity -- the header now spells out explicitly why DETF alone, not some combination with DETJ0, is the right measure.
  • ELEM_MIN_DETF tracks the worst (smallest) DETF over the element's 8 Gauss points per increment, same pattern as ELEM_MAXABS_ENG for the existing trigger.
  • Failure check at end of increment (unchanged timing): engineering-strain trigger checked first, Jacobian trigger second (ELEM_MIN_DETF <= JAC_FAIL .OR. ELEM_MIN_DETF <= 0, the <=0 branch is an explicit safety net independent of JAC_FAIL's sign, per the goal's instruction) -- same latch (svars(49)), same "freeze everything, go inert" dead state as before. Both trigger messages identify which one fired ((ENG STRAIN) vs (JACOBIAN)).
  • Audited every division/sqrt in the file for collapse-safety (per the goal's explicit instruction): all are already guarded (DMAX1(..., 0.0D0) before DSQRT, SIGMA_TRIAL .GT. SMALL before dividing by it, DABS(DETJ) .GT. SMALL in SHAPE8H_TL, and CHARLEN/OMEGA_MAX are built from the reference volume DETJ0, which never changes with deformation and so can't be driven to zero by element collapse). No new guards were needed.
  • nprops 10 -> 11 (*UEL Property data line fix from Stage 1 -- 8-per-line -- already accounted for in the new generator).

Decks (verification/make_t9_jacobian_trigger.py,

verification/roar/t9/) -- plasticity deliberately OFF (IPLAST=0) and EPS_FAIL=10 (unreachable), isolating the Jacobian path, same one-new-mechanism-at-a-time approach as every earlier stage.

  • t9_heavy_compression: same clean uniaxial-stress BC as T6/T7/T8, driven to u1=-0.97 (affine compression, F11 -> 0.03).
  • t9_shear_collapse: back face FULLY fixed (cantilever, not the minimal rigid-body-removal pattern -- this test wants a genuinely non-affine collapsing shape, not a clean analytic comparison), combined shear (u2=+4.0, several times the unit edge) and compression (u1=-0.8) on the driven face.

JAC_FAIL=0.05 (props(11)), the goal's suggested starting value, used as-is (not independently validated/tuned here).

False start: shear_collapse's first attempt never tripped

First attempt used u1=-0.6, u2=2.5. Job completed (rc=0) but with no VUEL_FAILURE message at all, and the .sta file's TOTAL ENERGY column went sharply negative (-4.99e8 by the final increment) -- a red flag. Added temporary per-call WRITE instrumentation (ROAR job 55972119, removed before the final run) and found min(det(F)) only reached 0.091 by the end of the ramp -- above JAC_FAIL=0.05 -- while max|eng strain| reached 1.55 (way past any physically meaningful small-strain range, but still under the deliberately-unreachable EPS_FAIL=10). Neither trigger failed to fire; the test simply didn't deform the element far enough to cross either threshold. The negative total energy is the St. Venant-Kirchhoff elastic law's known large-strain pathology (already documented in the file header: St.VK is accurate for moderate strains only) manifesting at eps~1.5, not a bug in the trigger logic. Fixed by increasing the excursion to u1=-0.8, u2=4.0.

Result: PASS, both decks

Both completed (rc=0, THE ANALYSIS HAS COMPLETED SUCCESSFULLY), no NaN/Inf/error anywhere in either .dat file.

t9_heavy_compression (ROAR job 55972126): trips via

*** VUEL_FAILURE (JACOBIAN): elem = 1  time = 9.1149081E-03

RF1 (summed over the four x-pinned nodes) history: builds from 0 to a peak of -3.85e10 Pa around eps=-0.42 (again, the StVK large-strain pathology -- a real elastic law would never predict a 38.5 GPa stress on a 200 GPa-modulus material at 42% engineering strain; this is expected and already documented, not new), the magnitude naturally receding as eps continues toward -1 (the stress-strain curve for St.VK is non-monotonic at large compressive strain), then at the frame immediately after the trip (t=0.009375) drops to exactly 0.0 and stays there through eps=-0.970 at the end of the ramp.

t9_shear_collapse (same job): trips via

*** VUEL_FAILURE (JACOBIAN): elem = 1  time = 6.8083936E-03

RF1-sum grows (again non-physically large, StVK pathology, now compounded by the combined shear+compression state) to 4.47e11 Pa at the frame just before the trip, then exactly 0.0 at the very next frame and every frame after, through the end of the ramp (eps=-0.800, U2 continuing to move per the prescribed BC regardless -- the dead element no longer resists, exactly as designed).

Both confirm the three things Stage 3 set out to check: the trigger fires before det(F) reaches zero or goes negative (confirmed via the debug instrumentation during the false-start diagnosis, and no NaN/error ever appeared in either final run), the run completes without aborting, and RF goes to exactly zero immediately after the trip.

Open issues

  • The St. Venant-Kirchhoff elastic law's large-strain pathology (non-monotonic, physically-absurd stress at large strain) is real and already documented in the file header, but this stage is the first to actually witness its effect on the .sta energy balance directly (the sharply negative TOTAL ENERGY in the first shear_collapse attempt). It did not prevent the Jacobian trigger from working correctly once the deformation was large enough, but it does mean a JAC_FAIL threshold set too close to the StVK law's breakdown point would be racing an increasingly unreliable stress field -- worth keeping in mind if JAC_FAIL=0.05 is later revised away from the goal's placeholder value. Not a defect in this stage's work.
  • Same Stage 1/2 caveats carry forward (no direct SVARS access for a VUEL in Explicit; RF/U node history is the evidence channel).
