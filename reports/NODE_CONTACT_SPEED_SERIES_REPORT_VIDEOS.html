Node-contact impact-speed series

ROAR jobs 56386750 to 56386754 · branch vuel-plastic-failure · 14 Oct 2026 · deck Meshing/Flatv2.inp with mesher-generated plate node contact

Result. All five runs (100, 200, 300, 400 and 500 m/s) completed to 70 us with no solver errors, and the new plate node-contact surface passed the Abaqus datacheck. The ball keeps 93 to 97% of its speed at 70 us, and 3 to 9% of its kinetic energy is lost.

Islands. Surviving ghost islands appear only at the two lowest speeds (8 at 100 m/s, 33 at 200 m/s) and are zero from 300 m/s upward. No ball node ever sat inside a live ghost beyond a single node at 400 m/s.

Notes and caveats

  • The ball is at or near its lowest point below the plate at 70 us in every run, so it has passed through the plate even at 100 m/s while losing only about 3% of its energy. Whether that is physical for this material is for you to judge; the extraction measures it but does not check it.
  • The fiber node surface passed datacheck but has not been solved, so no run here exercises it.
  • The island count uses the existing rule (alive ghost within 10 mm of the axis with at least 3 neighbours, at least 60% of them deleted). At 100 m/s the crater is small, so 8 islands there are against 489 live ghosts nearby.
  • The plate-and-element extraction scripts are the ones used for the earlier flat_v2 runs, unchanged.
