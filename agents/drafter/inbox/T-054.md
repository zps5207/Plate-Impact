## Questions to answer, each with numbers
1. Where are the deleted hosts (per layer inside the ply, per ply, radial distance from the impact axis, depth below the top face) and when (deletion frame/time)? Are they inside the ball footprint (radius about 4.76 mm), or in an annulus (shear plug), or farther out?

What stress state did each deleted host have just before deletion: stress triaxiality (pressure/Mises, compression negative), sign of the maximum principal stress, and equivalent plastic strain history? The ODB (`PRESELECT` field output, frames every ~70 ns) holds S/PEEQ for the hosts; the viewer data does not.
3. Are the deleted hosts in the fiber-bearing middle layer or in the outer layers, and is deletion related to the fiber? Compare deletion fraction against per-element Vf (VUEL deck has it in SDV107 / `EMBMESH_VUEL_DIAGNOSTICS`; for the native deck recompute from the deck `Meshing/newvals-paper-tt3/vuel/nv_vuel.inp` initial-solution blocks). Check the axial strain/stress in the fiber elements next to deleted hosts: are fibers carrying tension while the surrounding host dies in compression/shear?
4. Delamination timing: at the times host deletion is accelerating (use the status counts), what is the CSDMG state of the cohesive interfaces above and below (CSDMG is written by `*Contact Output`)? Has the interface failed before, at, or after the hosts under the ball are deleted? Does the plug (a column of deleted hosts with the same radius through many plies) form before any ply separates?
5. Compare native and VUEL: same questions, counts of deleted hosts, and whether the volume correction changes the picture.
