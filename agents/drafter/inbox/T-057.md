## Background (Hub-measured, 2026-11-10..12)
- T-056 was applied (with Hub fixes) and run on ROAR in `/scratch/zps5207/ctv/fh056/`. Correct keyword, accepted by Abaqus 2024:
  `*Surface Interaction, name=FiberHostBaseline, USER=INTERACTION, PROPERTIES=2, DEPVAR=2, TRACKING THICKNESS=0.0005` followed by a TRULY BLANK data line, then `2e+11, 0.01`.
- Full model `nv3_fh` (= nv3_int contact + fiber-host contact; 20 plies, ball 1028 m/s, 2 us): ETOTAL flat 469.2 J to 1.405 us, then 864 J at 1.48 us, 25,700 J at 1.9 us; stable dt 3.8e-8 -> ~1e-9; .sta warns about deep penetration (node sets InfoNodeDeepPenetAll / InfoEdgeDeepPenetAll).
- Bisect (same deck, 2 us):
  - A = only native `General` pairs fiber-ply-k vs host-ply-j (j != k) + ball pairs: STABLE (469.2 -> 468.8 J).
  - B = only the 20 own-ply pairs fiber-ply-k vs host-ply-k with FiberHostBaseline: BLOWS UP (1,018 J at 1.70 us, 2,113 J at 1.91 us, dt 2.6e-9).
  So the own-ply user interaction alone causes it.
- ODB diagnosis of nv3_fh (`Meshing/newvals-fiberhost-tt3/results/diag_nv3_fh.txt`, read it): the energy jump is all kinetic (ALLIE < 1 J). It sits in single orphan top-ply (PLY-20) nodes on the y=0 symmetry plane next to the impact centre: node 720 at 2.29e5 m/s (constant afterwards, i.e. all its elements were deleted, ~400 J on a ~1.5e-8 kg node), later node 10859 at 1.46e6 m/s. Deep-penetration nodes: FIBERS-PLY-20 at mid-ply (z = -0.635 mm) and PLY-20 host nodes. Ghost deletions begin in PLY-20 at ~1.33 us (18), 26 at 1.40 us, 446 at 2.0 us. The blow-up follows the first deletions.
- Order of magnitude that does not add up: k = 2e11 Pa/m times d <= 0.5 mm (tracking thickness) gives <= 1e8 Pa, times a facet area ~2e-7 m^2 gives ~20 N. Throwing a 1.5e-8 kg node to 2e5 m/s within ~0.08 us needs ~1e4-1e5 N. Something in the routine's inputs/outputs is far off from what we assume.
- Coupon diag from T-056 follow-up (`verification/roar/fiberhost_t056/coupon_fiberhost_diag.inp`, routine `vuinteraction_diag.for`): PENETRATION is negative for an open gap and positive for overclosure; Abaqus does NOT add the fiber node-surface thickness to it; the same slave node gets separate block entries per master facet. In that coupon nothing ever overclosed, so per-pair vs per-node state is STILL unknown.

## Hub hypotheses (test them; do not assume any is right)
H1. State is not per node-facet pair: when a contact point re-pairs to another facet (sliding, or erosion exposing INTERIOR faces), the stored d0 belongs to a different facet (or is reset at the wrong moment), so d - d0 jumps.
H2. Erosion exposes INTERIOR faces with fiber nodes already 0.2-0.4 mm behind them (inside TRACKING THICKNESS 0.5 mm, 18x the model default). Faces of heavily distorted/half-deleted elements (LE 1.7-5) give nonsense penetration, normals or AREAPROX.
H3. Contact forces still act on fiber nodes whose host element was deleted (embedded constraint state after host deletion is unknown), or on orphan nodes after nodal erosion.
H4. Our use of STRESS(2..3) incoming values / DRDISP for friction is wrong (e.g. DRDISP is total, not incremental, or incoming STRESS is not last increment's value), so tangential stress grows without bound before the mu*p cap applies, or the cap uses a wrong p.
H5. Return values are interpreted differently than we think (units, sign, whether STRESS is per unit area times AREAPROX, whether the stable-increment estimate ignores our stiffness).
