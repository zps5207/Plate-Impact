## Background

Project: embedded-fiber composite plate-impact meshing (`embmesh/`) with a node-coincident VUEL + ghost host (`embmesh/vuelghost.py`, `uel/VUEL_failure.for`, copies in `source/`). Every host hex becomes two elements on the SAME node labels: a `VU1` user element (`VUEL_failure.for`: the real stiffness and mass, with its own engineering-strain and Jacobian failure triggers) and a native `C3D8R` "ghost" whose material is the host material scaled by 1e-4 (stiffness, yield and density), with `*Damage Initiation, criterion=DUCTILE` at equivalent plastic strain 1e-6 and `*Damage Evolution, type=DISPLACEMENT` 0, so it deletes the instant its PEEQ reaches 1e-6. The ghost exists so the failed region disappears visually and takes part in contact. A VUEL cannot delete itself (confirmed on real Abaqus). The 328 T3D2 fibers are `*Embedded Element` in the host elset.

## What the user sees and what is confirmed

User, verbatim: "there are definitely still ghost elements that should be deleting but are staying alive." Earlier they described ghosts near the crater that never delete while the ball passes through, and fibers pulling on elements far from the impact and failing them.

Run to investigate: **flat_v2**, ROAR job 56286114, Abaqus/Explicit 2024, deck `evidence/flat_v2_output.inp` (14 MB, the mesher output). Ball (Mild Steel, Johnson-Cook damage) 1 mm above a 10 mm plate (quarter model, XSYMM/YSYMM, plate edge encastre) at 1000 m/s, step 7e-5 s. General contact between element surfaces `Surf-1` (plate faces) and `Surf-2` (ball). Confirmed from the ODB (do not re-derive; read the files in `evidence/`):

- Within r <= 10 mm: 802 ghosts, 717 deleted, 85 alive. **58 are islands** (alive at the end, >= 60% of nearby neighbours dead). They sit at z = -2.5 to -7.5 mm (top face z = 0), mostly r = 8 to 10 mm. `island_peeq_v2.json` has one row per ghost within 10 mm.

So the ghost criterion could not have fired on them: they never yielded. The deleted ghosts read 0 PEEQ after deletion, so a deleted-versus-alive PEEQ comparison is NOT possible from this file.
