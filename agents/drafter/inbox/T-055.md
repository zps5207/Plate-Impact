- VUEL: 0 dead at 0 us, 795 at 0.49 us, 28,521 at 0.99 us, 32,246 at 1.56 us, 50,560 at 5.05 us, 97,668 at 100 us.

Dead hosts span radius 1 to 100 mm in the same plies, so most are nowhere near the ball. Mean per-element fiber fraction (SVAR107) of dead hosts equals that of alive hosts (0.146 in outer layers), so it is not Vf-correlated. (Layer split was computed from ghost-element centroids and the assumption that viewer host order = ply-major, ghost label order; verify that mapping first.)
- Solver log `slurm-56974552.out` (ROAR `nv3_vuel/`): 92,453 `*** VUEL_MATERIAL_FAIL` lines; 27,293 elements at times <= 1e-7 s, the first at t = 3.798e-8 s (first increment); 38,240 before 0.97 us.
Ruled out by Claude: ply overlap or gap (ply thickness 0.00126999998 m vs translation 0.00127 m, gap 1.5e-11 m), ball overlap (initial gap 1 mm, velocity only on BALL_RP), datacheck overclosure messages (none in `nv3_vuel.dat`). Not checked: cohesive (CSDMG) damage over time, whether the earlier 1-layer VUEL runs (`ctv/bt3/vrb_vuel`) had this.

## Where to look
`uel/VUEL_material.for`: the matrix model (Stage 8) fails an element when ELEM_D_MIN >= 1 - 1e-6 (all 8 Gauss points, around line 940-960); damage is evolved around lines 735-770 with CHARLEN = ELVOL_REF^(1/3), UBAR_F = 2 Gf / SIGY0_ONSET, D_TRIAL = CHARLEN (PEEQ - PEEQ_ONSET) / UBAR_F. Reaching D = 1 needs PEEQ of roughly 0.1 (onset) plus about 0.1 more, within the first increment, which is implausible physically, so suspect initialisation or a coupling artifact: the `*Initial Conditions, type=SOLUTION` blocks (20 blocks, 159 values per element, only SVAR107 nonzero), `ELVOL_REF` and how it is set, the element mass and stable-step scaling with eta = max(1 - Vf, 1e-6), the ghost C3D8R hosts and their coupling, the Ply-k.fix ENCASTRE clamp, the cohesive contact. Also compare what is different from the 1-layer VUEL decks that worked, and why ply 13 stays clean.
