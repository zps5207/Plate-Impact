The Evolution of Project Knowledge: Decision Chain Summary

This master timeline tracks how the understanding of composite impact physics, numerical stability, and projectile dynamics evolved across 44 days of high-performance explicit FEA modeling on Penn State ROAR:

  1. VUEL Foundation (Oct 1–6): Developed and verified user-defined 8-node hex element with J2 plasticity and failure triggers (Stages 0–6), establishing ghost-element synchronization.
  2. Node-Contact & Rim Island Workarounds (Oct 12–14): Solved surviving ghost elements in Flat v2 by introducing node-based plate contact. Characterized velocity scaling from 100 to 500 m/s.
  3. Plywise Laminates & Contact Thickness Pathology (Oct 16–23): Moving from monolithic plates to 20-ply laminates (vp3/vp4) revealed that assigning artificial contact thickness to all ply nodes creates massive kinetic energy blowups (+73% ALLKE) and premature host deaths.
  4. Eroding Element Contact (vp5, Oct 23): Eliminated artificial energy creation by migrating plate-ball contact to an eroding element-based surface (EMBMESH_PLATE_ELEMS).
  5. The Inversion Resolution & Ball Test Suites (Oct 26–31): Audited apparent ball 'inversion' in bt1/bt2. Extraction of element STATUS proved that 99.8% of inverted hexes were already deleted ghost elements. Rigid projectile modeling adopted as standard (bt3).
  6. Projectile Geometry Error Discovery (Nov 2): Discovered root error in Vals.inp: projectile was 3/4-in diameter instead of the experimental 3/8-in diameter (8× mass error). Corrected in vrb_native/vrb_vuel.
  7. Through-Thickness Meshing & Overclosure (Nov 5–6): In 3-elements-per-ply meshing (NV3), node contact overclosed cohesive interfaces (27,000 dead hosts in inc 1 of nv3_smear). Resolved with element contact in nv3_elem (100 μs in 13.3 h).
  8. Fiber-Host Contact Resolution (Nov 10–13): Attempted own-ply fiber-host contact via VUINTERACTION in nv3_fh, causing energy explosion. A/B bisection and cluster coupons (cx057b) proved proximity state is not fresh per facet. Dropped own-ply contact, stabilizing production candidate nv3_fhA100.
