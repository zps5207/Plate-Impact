Vals Projectile Geometry Correction: Resolving the 3/4-inch vs 3/8-inch Discrepancy (vrb_native & vrb_vuel)

Author: Drafter (T-060) · Date: 2026-11-02 · Decks: Meshing/vals-plywise-ballfix/ · ROAR directory: ctv/bt3/vrb_native and vrb_vuel

Discovery of the 8× Projectile Mass Error:
On 2026-11-02, an audit against Valerie's experimental paper (References/Valerie's Paper.pdf, HB26 Dyneema plate impact) revealed a fundamental discrepancy in the root input deck Meshing/Vals.inp. The Ball part in Vals.inp was defined with a radius of $R = 9.525$ mm ($3/8$ in radius, i.e. $3/4$ in diameter). The experiment and paper explicitly specify a 3/8-inch diameter ball ($R = 4.7625$ mm). Because spherical volume scales as $R^3$, all previous Vals-derived models (vp3, vp4, vp5, bt1, bt2, bt3 d1–d7, vr_native) used a projectile with eight times the true mass and kinetic energy (3546 J vs 469 J in quarter model).

1. Purpose & Physical Impact

The purpose of the vals-plywise-ballfix study was to correct the projectile geometry to the true experimental 3/8-inch diameter, re-center the projectile with the proper 1.5 mm standoff, apply the experimental impact velocity of 1028 m/s, and evaluate plate penetration response in both native C3D8R and Stage 7 VUEL models.

5. Findings & Architectural Impact

  • Host Deletion Drops Under Realistic Mass: Host element deletion dropped from 2.12% (1,704 cells in 3/4-inch d6 at 70 μs) to 0.76% (611 cells in 3/8-inch vrb_native), reflecting the lower momentum of the true 3/8-inch sphere.
  • Host Deletion Numbers: Native C3D8R deletes 611 of 80,500 host cells (0.76%). VUEL deletes 1,222 of 161,000 total sub-elements (each host cell is represented by 2 sub-elements: 1 VUEL and 1 ghost C3D8R), corresponding to 611 host cell pairs (0.76%).
  • Energy Dissipation Differences: VUEL exhibits higher total energy loss (-20.1% vs -6.3% in native). This difference remains unexplained / open.
  • Adoption as Project Standard: The 3/8-inch rigid ball at 1028 m/s was adopted immediately as the mandatory baseline for all subsequent NewVals (NV3) decks.

6. What Changed Next

With the projectile geometry corrected, the project advanced to 3-elements-per-ply meshing (T-053). When these 3-layer decks were run with node-based contact, an unexpected pre-impact overclosure pathology emerged, leading to NEWVALS_NV3_CONTACT_EVOLUTION_REPORT.html.

7. Not Verified / Open Questions

  • Fibers did not delete in either 70 μs run (0 of 206,930), indicating that 70 μs captures the initial indentation and delamination phase rather than full plate exit.
  • The original vr_vuel chain (56950156) was cancelled before completion once the ball error was found.
