# T-025
role: refiner
round: 1
goal: Rework of T-024 (curved `stacked` fibers). Replace the "vertex at every host-edge crossing + merge short segments" approach with EVEN node distribution along each fiber, which is what the flat layup already does. This fixes the 33.8 um shortest segment without special merging.

## Why
The user's instruction: short fibers should be solved by distributing the nodes evenly across the fiber length, not by choosing a length and stepping along the fiber one segment at a time. Segment lengths then differ from fiber to fiber, but within one fiber they are all equal.

`flat_disc_fibers` in `embmesh/fibers.py` already does exactly this: after clipping a row to the host outline it computes `n_segments = max(1, round(clipped_span / target_length))` with `target_length = cbrt(median host volume)`, and places `np.linspace(lo3, hi3, n_segments + 1)` nodes along the row. That is why the flat run's shortest segment is 918 um. Your T-024 `stacked` code instead put a vertex at every in-plane edge crossing of the reference face and then merged vertices within 5% of an edge, which left 33.8 um segments.

## What to change (outbox/T-025/, mirroring the project layout, built on your T-024 files)
