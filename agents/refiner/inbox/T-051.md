# T-051
role: refiner (codex)
round: 1
goal: From the existing ball-test data, find out WHY the steel ball's hex elements keep inverting (negative Jacobian) before or instead of being deleted: which elements go first, where, when, what loads them, and which numerical mechanism is responsible.

## Context
Native 20-ply Vals quarter-disc deck, 1000 m/s steel ball (C3D8R + 1,500 C3D6R wedges, 18,500 elements), Abaqus/Explicit, SI units. bt1 (b1-b6) and bt2 (c1-c3, at 1000 and 500 m/s) are 12 us runs that vary one thing at a time. In every deck the ball erodes from ~1.5-1.9 us (about 80-90 elements/us) and 1.5-3.9% of ball hexes have a non-positive corner Jacobian by 12 us. The user's read: elements invert and blow up before they delete. Nothing tried so far reduces it: beam vs truss fibers, JC damage D4 fix, max degradation 0.8, steel card variants (A, 316L), a new uniform O-grid ball mesh (b2). The new ball mesh is 3x faster but did not stop it.
Known facts: the JC damage card has D4 = -0.05279 (T-049 showed the failure strain goes negative above ~1.7e4 /s; b4/c-decks set D4 = +0.002); JC damage evolution displacement is 0 (abrupt fail); ball contacts host through an eroding element surface and fibers through node contact with THICKNESS 0.618 mm (T-050: that is 3.7x the smallest ball element). Prior analysis: `agents/refiner/outbox/T-050/BALL_FAILURE.md` (and its concern: the ODB energy labels are mJ per the extract, but SI geometry suggests kJ-scale; settle it).

## Deliverable (outbox/T-051/)
1. `analyze_inversion.py` plus a short `INVERSION_FINDINGS.md` that answers, with numbers:
   - Which ball elements invert first (position in the ball: nose, rim, along the symmetry planes, wedge vs hex, the O-grid core vs the outer layer; relation to the nodes touching the contact surface), and at what time relative to the first deletion.
   - Does inversion precede deletion in the same elements, or do elements invert after their neighbours delete (loss of support, hourglass modes, freed nodes with no stiffness)? Check nodes whose all attached elements are deleted.
   - Is it the ball mesh (element aspect ratio, minimum edge length vs contact thickness), hourglass (C3D8R reduced integration), contact (nodes pushed by the 0.618 mm fiber node contact into the ball), or material softening? Rank the candidates with the evidence for each.
   - How the inversion fraction and first-inversion time scale with impact speed (1000 vs 500 m/s, c-decks) and with ball mesh (b2 vs the rest).
2. A ranked list of 3-5 concrete fixes with the evidence for each (for example: element deletion on distortion, enhanced hourglass control, finer or different ball mesh, contact thickness, node-contact vs element-surface for fibers, ball material card), and which of them can be tested with a deck change alone.
3. If the data cannot discriminate, say exactly which extra ODB field (element-level quantities, contact forces) is needed and write the extraction script.
