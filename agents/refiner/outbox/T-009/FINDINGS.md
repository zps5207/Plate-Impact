## Conclusion

**Verified from the supplied deck and ODB extracts; high confidence:** the 58 surviving ghost islands have not initiated the native ductile damage law. All 58 remain `STATUS=1` across the 101 sampled frames, have `PEEQ=0` throughout, and have sampled peak Mises stress at most 60.345, below the ghost material's initial yield stress of 61.1. The ghost damage law is driven by accumulated plastic straining; these elements have not yielded. The 538 sampled dead adjacent ghosts all reach `STATUS=0`. The ghost can therefore remain visible beside deleted neighbors without a faulty deletion switch. (`island_history_v2.json`; `flat_v2_output.inp:451799-451809`.) PEEQ cannot establish whether the paired VU1 failed; its SDVs are absent from the ODB.

**Hypothesis; moderate confidence:** the two coincident elements have different failure measures and integration rules. VU1 tests maximum absolute engineering strain and minimum determinant of F over eight Gauss points, at end of increment; the C3D8R ghost tests plastic damage at its reduced integration point. A VU1 can meet its strain/collapse limit while its ghost remains elastic. In this deck, VU1 `EPS_FAIL=0.113800332690901` and `JAC_FAIL=0.05`, while the ghost's first yield stress divided by Young's modulus is `61.1/508=0.12027559` (a uniaxial elastic strain comparison only). Hydrostatic compression may distort a ghost with little Mises stress and no PEEQ. This is a plausible mechanism, **not** proof that any of the 58 paired VU1 elements failed. (`VUEL_failure.for:628-642,913-938`; `flat_v2_output.inp:58395-58400,451799-451809`.)

## VUEL force after failure

**Verified from code; high confidence:** the failure latch is set after the current increment's force has been assembled, so that increment gets its full force. On the next call, `D` advances by `dtimeCur/DEGRADE_TIME` if a positive ramp is configured. Internal force contains `(1-D)` times the elastic/plastic and numerical-viscosity stresses, plus `D` times a compression-only hydrostatic residual. The residual is `r K min(tr(Ee),0)` on the three normal stress components; it has no tensile response. With positive `r`, a fully degraded element can continue to carry compressive force indefinitely while `tr(Ee)<0`. (`VUEL_failure.for:552-580,821-863,913-949`.)

**Verified for this deck; high confidence:** its VU1 property record has `DEGRADE_TIME=0` and `COMP_RESIDUAL_BULK_FRAC=0`. Thus after a VU1 latches failure, the next internal-force call uses `D=1`, zero intact stress and zero residual, in either tension or compression. Bulk viscosity and Rayleigh terms are also multiplied by `1-D`. The failed VU1 still retains mass, but cannot remain a stiff compressive element holding a neighboring ghost in its pre-failure state through internal force. (`flat_v2_output.inp:58395-58397`; `VUEL_failure.for:552-580,667-680,821-863,938-949`.) This does **not** rule out mechanical support from other active elements or constraints.

## Abaqus deletion semantics

**Verified in the deck:** the ghost has one ductile initiation row `1e-6, 0, 0`, displacement evolution `0`, a C3D8R solid section linked to `EMBMESH_GHOST_CONTROLS`, and `ELEMENT DELETION=YES`. The scaled ghost has `E=508`, density `0.098`, and plastic yield `61.1` at PEEQ zero. (`flat_v2_output.inp:82399,451796-451809`.) The mesher copies the source material and scales the first density, elastic, and plastic columns; it overrides the initiation strain. (`vuelghost.py:216-250`.)

**Verified against [Abaqus ductile initiation documentation](https://docs.software.vt.edu/abaqusv2025/English/SIMACAECAERefMap/simacae-t-prpmechanicaldamageductile.htm) and [material data rules](https://docs.software.vt.edu/abaqusv2025/English/SIMACAEMATRefMap/simamat-c-materialdata.htm); high confidence for the documented behavior, version caveat below:** ductile initiation uses accumulated equivalent plastic strain and can depend on stress triaxiality and plastic strain rate. With a single row, the fracture strain is constant across those independent variables under Abaqus's default constant extrapolation. The row's zero triaxiality and zero rate are coordinates of the one table entry, not additional gates requiring those values. An element with PEEQ remaining zero cannot meet its positive `1e-6` threshold. The scaled stiffness does not itself guarantee yielding: the yield stress is scaled by the same factor, and the stress state and deformation govern whether the one point reaches yield.

**Verified against [Abaqus damage evolution documentation](https://docs.software.vt.edu/abaqusv2025/English/SIMACAEMATRefMap/simamat-c-damageevolductile.htm) and [material failure rules](https://docs.software.vt.edu/abaqusv2025/English/SIMACAEMATRefMap/simamat-c-materialdata.htm); high confidence for the documented behavior:** zero failure plastic displacement causes instantaneous failure once initiation is reached. Characteristic length converts *post-initiation* plastic strain into displacement for a nonzero evolution law; zero displacement is explicitly permitted and does not require a positive displacement increment. With element deletion enabled, the one integration location of a reduced-integration C3D8R can delete when its material point fails. No extra multi-point averaging hurdle is apparent here. The first condition is still plastic damage initiation, which the islands did not reach.

The retrieved official documentation is for Abaqus 2025; direct Abaqus 2024 pages returned access errors. The deck ran in Abaqus/Explicit 2024, and the supplied ODB observations are consistent with the documented behavior. Exact 2024 wording and a controlled 2024 single-element reproduction were **not** checked. No conclusion here relies on an uncited memory-only deletion rule.
